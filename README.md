### Project Overview

Analysis of 42 unique Viva Engage posts published between September and November 2026.

The goal was to evaluate employee communications performance, identify meaningful findings, and recommend practical improvements for the communications team.

### Key Findings

**1. Employee Reach**
- Organization-wide announcements reached 64.8% of employees on average, compared with 19.9% for standard posts.
- Even announcements did not reach every employee, highlighting the need to evaluate follow-up for critical updates.

**2. Click Activity**
- Two announcements with similar reach showed substantially different click activity.
- Link clicks indicate interaction but do not confirm that employees completed the requested actions.

**3. Campaign Reminders**
- Reminders generated more clicks per viewer in both HR campaigns but reached fewer employees.
- Higher click rates did not always result in more total clicks.

### Recommendation

Test a more focused communication process in the next two campaigns requiring employee action:

1. **Before launch:** Define the required action, target audience, deadline, and success measure.
2. **Before the deadline:** Review pending actions and coordinate targeted reminders with the responsible team.
3. **After the campaign:** Compare completion rates with reach and click activity to evaluate results.

### Further Investigation

- Actual enrolment and survey completion rates.
- Audience overlap between launch posts and reminders.
- Differences in reach across similar topics, audiences, and post formats.
